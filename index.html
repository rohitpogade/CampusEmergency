ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology for a Safer Campus

CampusEmergency is a student project concept designed to make campus issue reporting easier. It brings complaint submission, basic status tracking and emergency alert recording into one interface.

Institution: Priyadarshini College of Engineering

Purpose: Improve awareness, reporting and communication around campus concerns.
